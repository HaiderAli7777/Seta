INVOEEZ — ENTERPRISE EDITION 3.3 — QUICK START

TRY IT
1. Extract this ZIP and open invoeez.html in Chrome or Edge.
2. You are in the SAMPLE COMPANY (Zenith General Trading): a full year of
   invoices, bills, payments, stock and reports. Try anything — it is not real.

FIND YOUR WAY (3.23)
- Settings > Navigation: choose Sidebar (a menu on the left, the default)
  or Apps dashboard (no sidebar: Home shows the search box and every app,
  and each app has its own menu across the top).
- In the apps dashboard, Create new is at the end of the search bar on Home;
  inside an app, Create sits next to the search button at the top right.
- The dotted button at the top left (or g then h) brings you back Home;
  click the app's name to jump to another app.
- Every app's menu reads the same way: Overview, its work and records,
  then Reporting, then Configuration.

THE DASHBOARD (3.24)
- It reads like an invoice: the period and net profit at the top, progress
  through the period (click a quarter to look at it), the key figures, then
  tabs — Performance, Money in & out, Products & customers, Activity and
  Financial detail — and a profit & loss summary at the foot.
- Settings > Appearance > Colour theme: Ocean blue, Lagoon teal, Royal
  violet, Graphite or Desert sand.

OPEN A CUSTOMER, VENDOR OR PRODUCT FROM A DOCUMENT (3.26)
- On any quotation, invoice, purchase order, bill, receipt or expense, click
  the small arrow in the Customer or Vendor field (or their name under the
  document number) to open their record beside the document.
- On a product line, click the arrow on the line or "Open product" under it.
- Change what you need and Save changes (or Ctrl S): the draft follows -
  new terms move the due date, a new price reaches the lines (a price you
  typed stays). Esc closes; it asks first if you have unsaved changes.

COLOURS, COMPANIES AND BRANCHES (3.25)
- Each module uses the colour of its icon: Sales blue, Purchase violet,
  Inventory orange, Accounting green, and so on. Home and the dashboard keep
  your colour theme. Settings > Appearance > Module colours turns it off.
- Click the company button at the top: every company is listed with its
  branches under it. Pick All branches or one branch, or click a branch of
  another company to open that company on that branch.
- Several branches at once: on the dashboard, click branch chips to add or
  remove them; on reports, tick branches in the Branch list (Only picks just
  one). The dashboard, reports and lists all follow the same choice.
- Dashboard > Branches tab: the branches side by side, with Show only.

WHO CAN DO WHAT (3.23)
- Users & access > People > New user: pick a role (Accountant, Salesperson,
  Sales manager, Purchaser, Procurement manager, Storekeeper, Viewer or
  Administrator). The form shows exactly what that person will be able to do.
- Users & access > Roles: adjust a role (areas: None / View / Create & edit /
  Full; own documents only; hide costs; discount limit) or add your own.
- Users & access > Access rules: start from a ready-made rule (hide costs from
  sales, read-only auditor, no deleting, own documents only, no exports,
  fixed prices) and pick the people or roles. Rules only take rights away;
  administrators are never restricted.
- "Who can do what" and View as show it through each person's eyes. Each
  person's Home puts the apps for their job first, with their work for today.

COST CENTRES AND BUDGETS (3.23)
- Accounting > Configuration > Cost centres: one click adds Head office,
  Sales & marketing, Operations, Warehouse and Projects. Documents, expenses
  and journal lines then carry a Cost centre.
- Accounting > Budgets > Create a budget: start from this year's run rate or
  last year, adjust any month, then see Budget vs actual (Accounting >
  Reporting, or Reports).

START YOUR OWN COMPANY
3. Click "Start my company" (banner at the top, or Settings, or the user menu).
4. Enter your company name, TRN and your name. Choose what to keep:
   the chart of accounts, product categories and expense items are always kept.
5. Follow Workspace > Setup guide:
   - Company details and logo (Settings)
   - Review the chart of accounts and product categories
   - Import products and customers/vendors from Excel — templates are in the
     templates folder and inside the app. Opening stock and opening balances
     can be columns in those sheets.
   - Enter opening balances (bank, customers, vendors, stock)
   - Create your first invoice
   - Download a backup

KEEP YOUR DATA SAFE
- Everything is stored in this browser on this computer. Use Settings >
  Back up regularly and keep the file somewhere safe.
- Always open the same file at the same address/folder in the same browser.

WORK OFFLINE, SHARE THROUGH THE CLOUD (3.15)
- InvoEez saves every change on this device first, so it keeps working with
  no internet.
- To share one set of books between people and devices, run the InvoEez sync
  server (server/README.md). Then on each device open its address, go to
  Settings > Cloud sync, paste the access key and click Connect.
- Changes upload as soon as there is a connection. The top bar shows Synced,
  Offline · N waiting, or Syncing.

SEVERAL COMPANIES AND BRANCHES (3.18, 3.19)
- Click the company name at the top of the menu > Add a company. Each
  company has its own books, users, TRN, VAT return, backup and colour.
  Utilities & tools > Companies shows them all.
- Utilities & tools > Branches > Set up branches: three steps — head office,
  your other branches, then who works where and how documents are numbered
  (one series, or a series per branch such as INV/DRA/2026/0001).
- Working somewhere else today? Click the company name and pick the branch;
  it is marked Working here and new records go to it.
- Every report has a Branch filter. Reports > Branch performance compares the
  branches; Reports > Group report adds the companies together.

UPGRADING FROM 3.2
- Open the new file at the same address/folder in the same browser: your books
  load automatically with every customer, vendor and product kept.
- If they do not appear, use Settings > Restore with your backup file.
