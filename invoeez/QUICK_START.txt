INVOEEZ — ENTERPRISE EDITION 3.3 — QUICK START

TRY IT
1. Extract this ZIP and open invoeez.html in Chrome or Edge.
2. You are in the SAMPLE COMPANY (Zenith General Trading): a full year of
   invoices, bills, payments, stock and reports. Try anything — it is not real.

START YOUR OWN COMPANY
3. Click "Start my company" (banner at the top, or Settings, or the user menu).
4. Enter your company name, TRN and your name. Choose what to keep:
   the chart of accounts, product categories and expense items are always kept.
5. Follow Workspace > Setup guide:
   - Company details and logo (Settings)
   - Review the chart of accounts and product categories
   - Import products and customers/vendors from Excel — templates are in the
     templates folder and inside the app. Opening stock and opening balances
     can be columns in those sheets.
   - Enter opening balances (bank, customers, vendors, stock)
   - Create your first invoice
   - Download a backup

KEEP YOUR DATA SAFE
- Everything is stored in this browser on this computer. Use Settings >
  Back up regularly and keep the file somewhere safe.
- Always open the same file at the same address/folder in the same browser.

UPGRADING FROM 3.2
- Open the new file at the same address/folder in the same browser: your books
  load automatically with every customer, vendor and product kept.
- If they do not appear, use Settings > Restore with your backup file.
